Open roles

Direct Support Professional

$16 to $17 an hour · full time and part time

Help people with their day in our Cedar Rapids and Marion homes: cooking, errands, appointments, medication support, community life. Live in option at $225 a day, five days on and two off.

  • Diploma or GED, or two years of caregiving
  • Valid driver's license
  • Days, evenings and weekends available

Overnight Residential Support Staff

Starting at $16 an hour

The steady person on site through the night: keep the home safe and calm, help anyone who needs something, hand a clean note to the morning shift.

  • Same requirements as DSP
  • Works well for students and second jobs

Host Home Provider

Monthly compensation · contracted

One adult with a disability lives with you, family style. We train you, back you up around the clock and arrange scheduled respite. Compensation depends on the level of support and is tax free under federal rules for home providers.

  • No healthcare background required
  • Every adult in the home passes a background check
  • Home passes a LaBase safety review

Case Manager

Starting at $22 an hour, depending on experience

Keep each person's support plan current, be the point of contact for families and MCO coordinators, keep documentation tight across our homes.

  • Bachelor's in a human services field, or equivalent experience
  • Iowa Medicaid HCBS experience is a plus

Remote Support Professional

$18.50 an hour days · $20.75 evenings and weekends

Support our members over video from our Cedar Rapids support center and send a caregiver out when hands on help is needed. Office based, not work from home. We train and certify you.

  • Comfortable with a computer and video calls
  • Valid driver's license
